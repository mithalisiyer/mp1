iReserve

Designed a platform where students can reserve a box of food from dining halls after regular hours are over.

iReserve

Overview

iReserve is a project I developed as part of my Human-Computer Interaction class to address a significant issue faced by university students: managing mealtime amidst a packed schedule. Inspired by my own struggles, the iReserve system aims to streamline the dining hall experience by enabling students to pre-select meals and schedule pick-ups, ensuring they can access nutritious food quickly and efficiently.

Venmo Fundraising Prototype

Developed a prototype for a fundraising feature for either individuals or charities on Venmo using Figma.

Venmo Fundraising Prototype

Overview

Fundraising using multiple apps can quickly become confusing as there is a disconnect between promotion and donations on different platforms. People looking to create fundraisers or donate to them often find it difficult to advertise or find a cause to support. We created a prototype for Venmo Fundraising which streamlines the donation and fundraising process by allowing users to come to one place and complete these actions. With the already social money transfer aspect of Venmo, users can showcase their fundraisers to their friends and search for others to sponsor. This project explores how Venmo can make donations more seamless, trustworthy, and socially connected.

LifeSync

Created an app that streamlines health management tasks such as tracking appointments and sharing test results, fostering seamless collaboration within families.

LifeSync

Overview

LifeSync is a mobile app designed for young family caregivers managing the health of elderly family members, ensuring everyone stays “on the same page.” The app streamlines health management tasks such as tracking appointments, sharing test results, and emphasizing critical health alerts, fostering seamless collaboration within families.

About Me

I’m excited to connect with fellow designers, share ideas, and contribute to the ever-evolving landscape of UX/UI design.
